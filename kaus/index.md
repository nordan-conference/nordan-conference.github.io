**KAUS: Complex Analysis Without Seniors** is an event designed for graduate students, held just before Nordan. It provides an opportunity for graduate students in complex analysis or geometry to present their research to their peers. Participation is open to all graduate students, whether or not they wish to give a presentation. However, the key rule is that **no senior researchers are allowed**. This event is exclusively for graduate students or recent graduates, aiming to create a supportive space for the younger generation of complex analysts to practice their presentation skills and exchange ideas.

The event will take place on May 22-23 in Reykjavík, beginning at 4:00 PM on May 22. The talks will be held at the University of Iceland. The detailed schedule will be posted after registration is completed. On Thursday evening, there will be plenty of time for socializing and networking.
