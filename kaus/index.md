**KAUS: Complex Analysis Without Seniors** is a special event for graduate students. It offers an opportunity for graduate students in complex analysis or geometry to present their research to peers in a supportive environment. Participation is open to all graduate students, regardless of whether they wish to give a presentation. However, the rule is that **no senior researchers are allowed**. This event is exclusively for graduate students or recent graduates, creating a platform for the next generation of complex analysts to hone their presentation skills and exchange ideas in a welcoming environment. The tradition of KAUS was revived in 2024, after having been dormant since 2011. 

KAUS 2026 will take place on May 22 in Joensuu, just before the Nordan conference. The talks will be held at the University of Eastern Finland. The detailed schedule will be posted after registration is completed. There will be ample time for socializing and networking.

The organizers of KAUS 2026 are:
 - Jouni Rättyä, University of Eastern Finland
 - Risto Korhonen, University of Eastern Finland
 - Janne Gröhn, University of Eastern Finland
 - Atte Pennanen, University of Eastern Finland
