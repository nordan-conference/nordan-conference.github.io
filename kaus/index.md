**KAUS: Complex Analysis Without Seniors** is a special event for graduate students. It offers an opportunity for graduate students in complex analysis or geometry to present their research to peers in a supportive environment. Participation is open to all graduate students, regardless of whether they wish to give a presentation. However, the rule is that **no senior researchers are allowed**. This event is exclusively for graduate students or recent graduates, creating a platform for the next generation of complex analysts to hone their presentation skills and exchange ideas. The tradition of KAUS was revived last year, after having been dormant since 2011. 

KAUS 2025 will take place on May 22-23 in Reykjavík, just before the Nordan conference. The talks begin at 4:00 PM on May 22 and and will be held at the University of Iceland. The detailed schedule will be posted after registration is completed. There will be ample time for socializing and networking in the evening.
