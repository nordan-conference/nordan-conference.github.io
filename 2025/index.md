# University of Iceland

The 26th Nordan conference was held May 23rd to 25th at Hotel Stracta in Hella, Iceland. 
The excursion was a trip to Þórsmörk with a hike to Valahnúkur and a stop at Seljalandsfoss on the way back.

KAUS 2025 took place May 22nd and 23rd and was held at the University of Iceland in Reykjavík. 
The event was organized by Mar Saiz Aparicio from the University of Stavanger and Tryggvi Kalman Jónsson from the University of Iceland.
The evening was spent in the hot tubs in Sundhöllin in rainy weather.

The organizers of Nordan 2025 were:
- Benedikt Steinar Magnússon, University of Iceland
- Álfheiður Edda Sigurðardóttir, IMFM Ljubljana
- Bergur Snorrason, University of Iceland
- Gianmarco Brocchi, University of Iceland
