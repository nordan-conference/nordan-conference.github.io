# Nordan 2024

The 25th Nordan conference was held in May 3rd to 5th at the Östanskär Hotel & Conference Centre near 
Sundsvall, Sweden. 

For this occasion KAUS, Complex Analysis Without Seniors, was revived.  
This event took place on May 2nd and 3rd, and was organized by 
Álfheiður Edda Sigurðardóttir and Bergur Snorrason. 

Organization:
- Andreas Lind, Sundsvall University
- Per Åhag, Umeå University
