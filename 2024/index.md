# Mid Sweden University and Umeå University

The 25th Nordan conference was held in May 3rd to 5th at the Östanskär Hotel & Conference Centre near Sundsvall, Sweden. 

For this occasion KAUS, Complex Analysis Without Seniors, was revived.  
This event took place on May 2nd and 3rd, and was organized by 
Álfheiður Edda Sigurðardóttir and Bergur Snorrason. 

Organization:
- Jakob Hultgren, Umeå
- Andreas Lind, Sundsvall
- Aurélio Menegon, Sundsvall
- Antti Perälä, Umeå
- Egmont Porten, Sundsvall
- Per Åhag, Umeå
