title: Singular metrics on holomorphic vector bundles
abstract: Let $E\to X$ be a holomorphic vector bundle over a complex manifold $X$. I will discuss how one can define Chern form, Segre form and curvature tensor, associated with a class of singular metrics on $E$. I will also present some recent results in joint works in progress with Kalm, L\”ark\”ang, and Sera.
