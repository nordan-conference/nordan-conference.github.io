title: Exposing type results for smoothly bounded (strictly) pseudoconvex domains
abstract:  It is well known that every boundary point of a strictly pseudoconvex domain admits an exposing mapping. We shall discuss the difficulties, possibilities, and tools that appear when trying to extend this kind of result beyond the class of strictly pseudoconvex smoothly bounded domains.
