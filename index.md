## **Nordic Complex Analysis Meeting** <br>
*May 22–24, 2026 | Joensuu, Finland*

We are pleased to announce the 27th iteration of Nordan, the annual Nordic conference on complex analysis. In 2026, for the first time in its history, the Nordan conference will be held in Finland. The event will take place from May 22 to 24, at the Joensuu campus of the University of Eastern Finland. In connection with Nordan, a KAUS meeting for graduate students will be organized on May 22, 2026. For more information about KAUS, visit [here](https://nordan-conference.github.io/kaus/).
